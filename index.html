CORE SYSTEM

1. ASTRIV Central Platform (astriv.co)

Open astriv.co

The master administrative and corporate platform serving as the root umbrella for all ASTRIV initiatives. Engineered with a full-stack Node.js and SQL foundation wrapped in responsive Tailwind design, it manages enterprise operations, public digital identities, client intake, and global organization metrics.

Central Authentication
Unified session handling, role-based access control (RBAC), and secure administrative consoles across organizational tiers.
Statutory Compliance
Automated corporate accounting layouts, schedules 9230/3000 reporting, and audit verification ledgers for registered corporate oversight.
Modular Service Dispatch
Central routing hub directing institutional traffic, web applications, and developer API consumers to dedicated micro-services.
INTELLIGENT AGENTIC CORE

2. Gradentia AI (gradentia.astriv.co)

Open gradentia.astriv.co

Gradentia AI is a career counseling and academic intelligence platform designed to replace obsolete standardized aptitude screening with context-aware LLM reasoning. It systematically maps individual cognitive strengths, technical competencies, and market demands to generate dynamic career trajectory models.

LLM Skill Mapping
Dynamic skill gap forecasting that analyzes user performance against emerging market requirements and university admission criteria.
Autonomous Roadmaps
Step-by-step modular learning sequences, recommended certifications, and milestone tracking customized per learner profile.
Inquiry-Based Guidance
Replaces rote memorization paths with inquiry-driven conceptual discovery and analytical reasoning metrics.
OFFLINE-FIRST CAMPUS ERP

3. ASTRIV School Management System (school.astriv.co)

Open school.astriv.co

A comprehensive, offline-first institutional management suite engineered to maintain full operational continuity regardless of connectivity outages. Centralizes student profiles, double-entry fee ledgers, automated date sheets, staff attendance rosters, and structured assessment rubrics.

Offline-First Sync
Local-first client caching with seamless bidirectional database sync to remote MySQL instances upon reconnection.
Automated Fee Ledgers
Strict double-entry fee management, receipt generation, invoice reconciliation, and defaulter tracking pipelines.
A4 Layout Engine
Dynamic generation of print-perfect A4 exam date sheets, staff attendance registers, bilingual worksheets, and circulars.
